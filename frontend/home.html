Welcome to the Blockchain Notary System

About the System

The Blockchain Notary System allows you to securely notarize your important documents on the blockchain, ensuring their authenticity and integrity. Once notarized, your documents are tamper-proof and verifiable by anyone.

How It Works

Upload your document to the system to create a unique digital fingerprint stored on the blockchain. This fingerprint acts as proof of existence and timestamp for your document. You can later verify the notarization status of any document by uploading it for comparison.

Benefits

  • Secure and tamper-proof document notarization
  • Easy verification of document authenticity
  • Decentralized and transparent system
  • Fast and cost-effective notarization process
